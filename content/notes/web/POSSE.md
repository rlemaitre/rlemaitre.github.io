POSSE stands for **P**ublish on your **O**wn **S**ite, **S**yndicate **E**verywhere. It's a practice introduces by [[notes/web/IndieWeb]] in which:
1. you publish content (articles, comments, photos, videos) on your personal website (under your personal domain)
2. Then, you publish (automatically if possible) on other sites (big silos like Facebook, Bluesky, Mastodon, Twitter/X, Instagram, ...)
